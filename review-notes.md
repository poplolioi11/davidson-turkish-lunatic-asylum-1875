# Review notes

Version 0.2.0. Source frames 1–20.

The initial reading and a second AI reading were made separately from the same fixed Yale / Wellcome images. The second reading used the images directly in overlapping crops, with neither the initial text nor an OCR transcript as input. Both cover all 20 source frames, including 10 frames with original print. We compared the printed lines after separating the running-head page numbers already stored as pagination metadata and aligning the structured editorial gap. The differences and the surrounding lines were checked against the images. There were six differences in the casing of printed small capitals. No other differences in article-body wording or punctuation remained after the line comparison. The two readings are AI-assisted and have no human or expert proofreading or measured error rate.

## Changes in v0.2.0

- Frames 3, 5 and 7, author line. Changed Edin. to EDIN. The printed abbreviation uses small capitals. The wrapper title ends with a comma and the title leaf and article title end with a full stop.
- Frame 7, opening article page. Removed the inferred 1 from the printed-page field. The frame remains in the same position and the next frame has a printed 2.
- Frames 9, 11 and 13, running heads. Changed John H. Davidson to JOHN H. DAVIDSON to represent the small capitals. The word by is in lowercase italics in these headings. On frames 8, 10, 12 and 14 the numeral is to the left of the running title. On frames 9, 11 and 13 it is to the right of the running name. The numerals are indexed separately. The final running title ends in a full stop. The three preceding running titles end in a comma.
- Frames 1, 19 and 20, copy-specific material. Identified the later front board, library slip and marbled rear board as paratext. On the original wrapper, frame 3, a diagonal mark over the border is tentatively read as Raymenton. The later marks are described in frame notes.
